## Bonus terms that decide if you get paid

Every Bybit EU promotion has its own terms, so read them in your account. These are the conditions that most often decide whether a sign-up bonus is actually paid:

**Verified account only.** Rewards go to accounts that passed identity verification. That's not a Bybit choice so much as an EU legal requirement.

**One person, one account.** Duplicate or shared accounts break the rules. Bybit EU can cancel rewards if it finds several accounts linked to the same person, device or payment method.

**Deadlines are strict.** A task completed one day after its deadline doesn't count. There's usually no manual exception.

**Minimum amounts.** Deposit and trade tasks often have a minimum. A trade below the threshold won't complete the task.

**Net deposits.** Some deposit tasks look at net deposits, meaning deposits minus withdrawals. Withdrawing straight after depositing can undo the task.

**Eligible assets and pairs.** Not every trade counts. Stablecoin-to-stablecoin pairs, for example, are often excluded from trading tasks.

**Coupon usage rules.** A coupon may only apply to a specific product or need to be used within a few days of being credited.

**Fair use.** Self-trading, wash trading or any attempt to game the system can lead to rewards being withdrawn and, in serious cases, account restrictions.

## Taxes on crypto bonuses in the EU

In many EU countries, crypto rewards like a sign-up bonus are taxable, often as income at the value on the day you receive them, and later sales can trigger capital gains tax. Rules differ a lot between countries. Keep a record of every reward you receive, export your transaction history regularly, and check with a local tax adviser if you're unsure. Under the EU's DAC8 directive, crypto service providers report user transactions to tax authorities, so it's worth getting this right from the start.
